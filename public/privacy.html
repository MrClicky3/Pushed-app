Privacy Policy

Overload · Last updated 16 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Overload ("Overload", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our gym logging and progress-tracking application (the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address and an encrypted password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, basic profile information (name and profile picture), and a unique Google account identifier — only what is needed to create and secure your account.
  • Profile information. A username, and any optional display name, bio, and avatar you choose to add.
  • Workout data. The exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, body-weight entries, and related notes you log.
  • Social and competition data. Friend connections, invite codes, competition participation, standings, and badges.
  • Device identifier. To let you use the app before signing in, we store a random identifier on your device so your data can later be linked to your account.
  • Diagnostic information. If you submit a bug report, we collect the details you provide and basic technical context to help us resolve the issue.

Some data — such as your workout routines, weekly schedule, and app settings — is stored only on your own device and is not uploaded to our servers.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To sync your workout history and profile across your sessions.
  • To power friend leaderboards, competitions, and badges you take part in.
  • To respond to bug reports and support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Google user data

Overload's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use Google sign-in solely to authenticate you and create your account; we do not use Google user data for any purpose other than providing and improving the Service, and we never sell it or transfer it to third parties except as needed to run the Service or as required by law.

4. Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us operate the Service:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage.
  • Vercel — application hosting and delivery.
  • Google — optional sign-in.
  • Resend — delivery of transactional email such as bug-report notifications.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

5. Data retention

We keep your account and workout data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us; we will delete it within a reasonable period, except where we must retain limited information to comply with legal obligations.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including row-level security and encrypted connections, to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact
